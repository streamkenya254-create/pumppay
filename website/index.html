FAQ

Questions farmers ask

What happens if I miss a payment?

The pump pauses until you top up. Nothing is lost — pay any amount and the controller switches back on immediately.

What is the difference between PAYG and PAYU?

Pay As You Go: small deposit, daily payments, and you own the system at the end. Pay As You Use: no ownership, you only buy pumping hours when you need water — ideal for seasonal farming.

Can I switch plans?

Yes. Contact us and we'll move your controller to the plan that suits you.

Does it work without internet?

The controller uses GSM (like a phone). It stores your credit locally so it keeps running even when the network drops.

Who maintains the system?

Servicing is included while you're on a plan. Report faults from the app and a technician is dispatched.
