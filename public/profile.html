PumpPay is a locally manufactured solar pump controller that switches on when the farmer pays by M-Pesa. Farmers either pay daily until they own the system (Pay As You Go) or buy only the pumping hours they need (Pay As You Use).

Who we are

About PumpPay

PumpPay is developed by Tafiti Research & Innovation Hub, a Kenyan engineering and innovation team working in solar energy, embedded electronics and IoT.

We pair hardware we build ourselves with a payments and monitoring platform: a controller on the farm, a client app on the farmer's phone, and an admin dashboard for our field team.

The problem

Why it matters

  • Diesel pumping is expensive and prices keep rising.
  • A full solar pumping system costs too much to pay for upfront.
  • Imported controllers are hard to repair and get support for locally.
  • Suppliers have no simple way to finance farmers and collect payments.

Mission

To make reliable solar irrigation affordable for every farm by letting farmers pay in small amounts, as they grow.

Vision

To be East Africa's trusted, locally made platform for pay-as-you-go solar water pumping.

What we offer

Products & services

Hardware

Smart Pump Controller

Software

Client App & Dashboard

Android app to pay and track balance, water and pump hours; web admin for customers, devices and payments.

Solutions

Complete Solar Kits

Pump kits, panels, drip kits and tank level sensors, sold for cash or added to a PAYG plan.

Business model

Two ways to pay

Pay As You Go

Pay daily until you own it
from KES 150 / day
  • Small deposit, then daily, weekly or monthly payments
  • System becomes the farmer's after full payment
  • Free servicing while on plan

Pay As You Use

Pay only for the hours you pump
from KES 40 / hour
  • Low installation fee, no long-term contract
  • Buy pumping hours in bundles; unused hours never expire
  • Ideal for seasonal farming; upgrade to PAYG any time

Starting prices. Final plan depends on pump size, water source and farm.

Target market

Who we serve

  • Medium and large-scale irrigation farmers moving off diesel or grid pumping.
  • Companies offering pumping solutions that want a controller with built-in PAYG payment collection.
  • Farmer groups, cooperatives and institutions with shared water sources.
Why PumpPay

Our value

  • Reliability: protection against dry-running, over-current and tampering.
  • Longevity: built for Kenyan field conditions and easy to repair.
  • Local presence: made in Kenya, with spares and technicians close to the farm.
  • Built-in finance: M-Pesa collection and remote lock and unlock.
